Biography

Welcome to my homepage!

My name is Shi Boao (in Chinese, 施博骜). My English name is Bowie. I am a Junior undergraduate from The University of Hong Kong, majoring in Computer Science. I am conducting research in InfoBodied Lab supervised by Prof. Yang Yanchao, in HKU Musketeers Foundation Institute of Data Science. Previously, I had my first experience of research in XLang Lab, working on LLM agent. My interest is to build autonomous robots agent who can help with our daily life. Currently, I am focusing on humanoid robots, especially in the field of human scene/object interaction generation. I am passionate about any new stuff. If there are any potential collaborations or opportunities, please feel free to contact me.
